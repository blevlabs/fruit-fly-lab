Every gate below inherits these rules:

1. **Preparation and identities:** record sex, age, strain, temperature, tissue/preparation, geometry, load, stimulus history, units and uncertainty, plus source/data/map/configuration/checkpoint hashes. A female, larval, skinned-muscle or tethered preparation can validate its stated reference model; transfer to this adult male requires separate correspondence and validation.
2. **Calibration versus holdout:** partition independent biological specimens, recordings or conditions before fitting. Keep untouched stimulus timings, doses, loads, poses or environments for the stated prediction. Split correlated repeated measurements by specimen/session, not arbitrary rows. For engineered capabilities, separate development/training, selection/validation where applicable, and final untouched tests. Never choose a motor gain, wiring assignment or acceptance margin from candidate behavior.
3. **Readouts and causality:** observe the whole relevant chain from physical input to sensory, neural, NMJ, force and physical consequences. Artificial stimulation is allowed in labeled assays. Teacher instructions, target labels and simulator answers are evaluation-only. Record passive, release-blocked and pathway-specific interventions where applicable; confirm that a targeted intervention preserves unrelated basic sensing and movement.
4. **Replication:** the biological unit is an independently sampled animal/preparation, with cells and trials nested within it. A simulation seed, parameter draw or repeated trial is not another biological specimen. Engineered learning uses independently initialized individuals; evolution uses independent evolutionary runs; communication uses independently trained pairs/cohorts. Prespecify sample sizes using source variation or a separate pilot; no sample size or numerical margin is fabricated here.
5. **Verdicts:** `PASS` requires the prespecified biological/error/effect margins and every stated causal/held-out comparison. `FAIL` means an executable, adequately resolved assay violated a criterion, including an implementation invariant or a controlled capability prediction. `INCONCLUSIVE` means the assay ran but its uncertainty, identifiability or preparation validity cannot decide the claim. `NOT EXECUTABLE` means an essential datum, accepted route, implementation prerequisite, fixed split or numerical acceptance margin is missing. Both latter verdicts keep the gate open. A negative experiment may finish as an experiment while its required capability remains unachieved.
6. **Numerical acceptance:** deterministic state/identity/isolation fixtures require their declared exact invariants. Continuous physical predictions require timestep/resolution comparisons with an error budget fixed relative to the independent measurement and claim. Existing 10-versus-5-µs checks do not qualify new mechanisms. A larger timestep or weaker margin cannot repair a failed biological prediction.

The tables specify the required comparisons and missing inputs. **Unless a row explicitly cites an accepted result, its status is open.** A paper or plausible source route is not an extracted calibration/holdout package. No biological or capability margin has been set by this inventory, so those gates remain `NOT EXECUTABLE` until their named comparison package and assay specification exist. Implementation fixtures may proceed independently.

## Adult-system coverage and exclusions

The inspected graph contained 167,216 retained neurons, 25,587,572 directed neuronal edges and 124,193,283 synapse contacts. The 815 motor rows and 17,937 sensory rows are annotation selectors, not a whole-animal census. The frozen brain receipt also retains **254 endocrine/efferent/ENS entries outside both selectors**: 94 `vnc_efferent`, four `cb_efferent`, eight `efferent_ascending`, four `efferent_descending`, 72 `cb_endocrine`, 22 `vnc_endocrine` and 50 `ENS`. Their presence in the graph does not establish a peripheral output, organ model or endocrine action. Their exact roles require adjudication, not reassignment to a convenient muscle. The number 254 counts those named classes only; it is not an exhaustive census of every possible boundary case.

The retained graph excludes annotation rows under the import rule; the frozen receipt records 11,864 excluded rows with status `Glia`, alongside other orphan/anchor/unclassified segments. These are annotation-row counts, not proof of complete cells or an exhaustive glial census. Glial/metabolic support and omitted peripheral tissue cannot be inferred from chemical neuronal edges. Its additional boundary record identifies eight retained `vnc_intrinsic` rows with entry-nerve annotations and 29 superclass-null entry-nerve fragments: two traced/retained IDs 527138 and 544325, plus 27 excluded orphans. These are adjudication cases, not newly confirmed sensory neurons. Named circadian-type entries likewise require M4 regulatory validation rather than automatic sensory routing.

## Remaining evidence requirements

Attach source-specific anatomy, mechanics, sensory and transmission reference results to the corresponding biological gates. Each package must name its preparation, exact missing data and supported comparison. An implementation check can pass while physiological or capability evidence remains absent. Persistence and isolated circuit-reference work do not depend on resolving every peripheral mapping, but do not close those mappings either.

The [roadmap](../roadmap.md) defines current priorities; the [status page](../status.md) and [public checks](../../tests/) identify current implementation guarantees. No required repertoire item is removed merely because another action is easier to demonstrate.
