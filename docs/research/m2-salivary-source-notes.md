# M2 adult salivary supply and dry proboscis evidence

This report records a historical study and its inspected model version. Numerical results below are not new package checks; see [current status](../status.md), the [roadmap](../roadmap.md), and the [public results index](../../results/README.md). Original artifact labels identify records in the historical provenance archive.

Status, 2026-09-11: **source investigation complete for this bounded pass; physiological calibration remains open**. No retrieved adult *Drosophila melanogaster* source establishes a salivary pressure boundary, secretion-rate law, usable storage volume, saliva rheology/wetting law, or initially dry food-canal filling trajectory. This is a statement about the recovered evidence, not proof that measurements do not exist.

The existing [whole-proboscis mechanics](whole-proboscis-mechanics.md) correctly describes its hydraulic initialization as explicitly primed. That numerical initial condition is not evidence for a naturally primed adult proboscis and does not validate dry-mouth feeding or unlimited saliva supply.

## Primary adult evidence recovered

| Source and conditions | What the source supports | Calibration boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Wells & Andrew 2015, Fig. 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4667400/); adult Canton-S females and males, CO2 anesthesia, dissected into PBS, fixed and stained with DAPI/phalloidin/WGA, confocal z-stacks at 1 µm intervals | One duct leading to each unbranched gland; duct-associated taenidia; apical surface and duct coincide within the gland. Five cells surround most of the secretory lumen. A striated multinucleated structure was observed near the proximal gland in some preparations. | Images have 10/20/50 µm scale bars, but no adult Drosophila lumen-radius/length distribution, fluid storage, pressure or secretion-rate series is tabulated. A widened proximal lumen is an observation, not a calibrated reservoir. The study's quantitative lobe/duct dimensions concern *Anopheles stephensi*. |
| [Riparbelli, Callaini & Dallai 1993](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8296309/), DOI 10.1016/0040-8166(93)90056-Q; adult and larval Drosophila fluorescence/confocal microscopy | Primary abstract confirms regionally distinct adult cytoskeletal organization, unlike the larval gland. | Abstract recovered; full text and exact adult dimensions/experimental conditions remain unavailable in this pass. It supplies no recovered numerical secretion boundary. |
| [Swale et al. 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28911739/), DOI 10.1016/j.pestbp.2016.11.005; [author-institution record](https://repository.lsu.edu/entomology_pubs/164/) | Salivary-gland Kir1 knockdown reduces ingested volume and increases feeding time. | [Li et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8960802/), Fig. 6 | Adult Drosophila cumulative sucrose consumption is measured over four days by CAFE after Kir-channel modulation. | Its direct **1.2 ± 0.3 nL saliva** result (Fig. 2) is from *Aedes aegypti*, not Drosophila; it is a collected volume, not a Drosophila flow rate or storage capacity. |
| [Manzo et al. 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3341050/), Figs. 1/3 | Adult Drosophila pumping is approximately 6 Hz; increased food viscosity primarily prolongs cibarial filling. The experiment also measures ingestion and ingestion per pump. | Food intake and pumping constrain eventual demonstrated transport. They do not independently determine saliva pressure, food-canal material properties, or dry-filling physics. Listed pure-sucrose viscosities are reference values at 20°C, not measured adult saliva viscosity. Historical source: `outputs/program/m2/internal-state/PMC3341050.txt`. |
| [Jürgens et al. 2024](https://academic.oup.com/genetics/article/228/2/iyae129/7750380), Figs. 26–27; Oregon-R SEM atlas | Six major pseudotracheal grooves per labial palp; publication-scale images of open/closed external mouthparts. | External SEM geometry does not measure hydrated internal lumen, advancing/receding contact angles, trapped air, or cavitation. A new image-derived dimension would require explicit picks, scale calibration and preparation uncertainty. |

The Wells study gives adult topology worth preserving, but the attached striated structure is not a license to create a new functional pump, motor identity, or pressure waveform. Its mechanical action, neural identity, force, and relationship to the already described suspensor muscle remain separate questions.

## Routes that did not supply calibration

- The [Virtual Fly Brain adult-duct entry](https://www.virtualflybrain.org/blog/2022/01/01/adult-salivary-gland-duct-fbbt_00003136/) reports roughly **7 µm diameter**. This is a curated secondary lead. [Miller 1950's primary record](https://flybase.org/reports/FBrf0007735.html) and [anatomical figure](https://flybase.org/reports/FBim0000045) were located, but the original descriptive page was not recovered. Whether 7 µm denotes outer duct or hydraulic lumen diameter is unverified. Do not put 3.5 µm into Poiseuille conductance from this entry alone.
- [FlyAtlas epithelial transcriptome analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3734111/) reports candidate adult salivary transporters/receptors. Its cited classic physiological secretion paper is explicitly *Calliphora* (Berridge, Lindley & Prince 1976, DOI 10.1242/jeb.64.2.311). Transcript enrichment neither measures secretion nor resolves the adult Drosophila source pressure.
- [Lehnert et al. 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5247492/) directly tests porous-surface uptake, but its fly species are *Musca domestica* and *Calliphora vomitoria*. Their pseudotracheal diameters are respectively 12.27 ± 0.59 and 15.79 ± 0.35 µm. The paper's approximately 1 kPa pressure is a cross-species fit estimate, while approximately 10 kPa is a scaling comparison; neither is a pressure measurement in adult Drosophila. Neither species' geometry or contact angle can be transferred as an adult Drosophila calibration.
- [The adult capillary-feeding protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12274911/), troubleshooting problem 6, describes occasional upward movement of 30 mM sucrose during recordings of at least four hours. Saliva changing viscosity/surface tension is the authors' proposed explanation, not a measurement of either property.
- Larval glue, prepupal apocrine secretion, mosquito saliva collections, and Malpighian-tubule fluid secretion were excluded. They are different developmental tissues, species, or organs. Butterfly liquid-bridge observations identify possible physical mechanisms to investigate, not demonstrated adult Drosophila initial conditions.

## Exact missing data and next sources

| Required datum | Why the current evidence does not replace it | Next source route |
|---|---|---|
| Adult gland/duct/common-duct/salivary-canal lumen lengths and cross sections, with variability and hydrated geometry | A 7 µm unspecified duct diameter and fixed confocal panels do not establish hydraulic resistance or liquid inventory. | Recover Miller pp. 440–441/451 and Riparbelli full text; seek original Wells Fig. 4 z-stacks plus age/preparation metadata. Trace and measure actual lumina before fitting resistance. |
| Saliva production versus time, stimulation and backpressure; baseline/stimulated limits and recovery | Kir perturbations change food ingestion, which combines neural drive, pump mechanics and gland effects. | Swale 2017 full text/supplements first; if no direct adult assay exists there, obtain cannulated/collected adult Drosophila secretion data with stimulus, temperature, sex, age and collection interval. |
| Usable salivary liquid volume and pressure–volume relation at feeding onset | Stained intracellular puncta and widened lumen do not quantify available saliva or constitutive compliance. | Calibrated adult gland lumen imaging and pressure/volume or depletion/refill measurements. Keep gland secretion separate from any crop-regurgitated fluid. |
| Adult saliva viscosity versus shear rate, surface tension and density; food/saliva mixture dependence | Pure-water or pure-sucrose values specify assay fluids, not saliva. | Adult saliva collection followed by fluid-property assays, with composition and temperature. |
| Advancing/receding wetting angles on adult hydrated pseudotrachea/food canal, roughness and continuity | SEM outlines show topology; the adult protocol only hypothesizes a saliva wetting effect. | Dynamic interface imaging using specified food/saliva on adult mouthpart cuticle. |
| Initial liquid/air distribution, filling-front trajectory, venting route, entrained gas and pressure failure/cavitation threshold | A primed circuit and steady Poiseuille law have no air–liquid interface. Adult pump frequency alone cannot identify those hidden states. | Direct adult Drosophila first-contact imaging with independently known starting hydration and pump phase; simultaneously measured or independently bounded pressure. |
| Absolute salivary outlet/pump pressure and pressure–flow response, including valve load geometry | No retrieved primary adult measurement supplies a salivary pressure boundary or stall load. | Adult micropressure/pressure-clamp data and matched gland/valve anatomy. The crop/food-pump estimate remains a distinct evidence stream. |

Until those inputs exist, a conservative finite-supply or two-phase numerical assay can be labeled an explicitly assumed physical experiment only. Passing conservation, dimensional checks, timestep checks, or a theory identity would establish numerical correctness; it would not close the salivary/dry-filling biological gate. No successful feeding outcome may be used to select the missing pressure, wetting, or storage parameters.

## Retrieval receipt

Full XML and readable text for Wells 2015, the FlyAtlas study, Li 2022, the adult feeding protocol and Lehnert 2017 are saved as `salivary-PMC*.xml/.txt` under `outputs/program/m2/internal-state/`. Source manifest (original artifact `program/m2/internal-state/salivary-retrieval.json`) records exact URLs, checksums and failures. Europe PMC's public full-text route succeeded. LSU direct retrieval returned 403; the 2017 DOI and a Nature IDP redirect were rejected by the web tool's URL security policy and were not retried. The legitimate independent PMC archive supplied the 2022 full text.

Sui et al. 2026 (DOI 10.1038/s41593-026-02412-y) supplies a separate cibarial physiology reference in [M4–M7 evidence](m4-m7-evidence.md). Its abstract alone does not identify an additional anatomical connection or timing rule.
