# M7: original Huang/Luo rate-model reference

This report records a historical study and its inspected model version. Numerical results below are not new package checks; see [current status](../status.md), the [roadmap](../roadmap.md), and the [public results index](../../results/README.md). Original artifact labels identify records in the historical provenance archive.

The bounded Python port reproduces **all 240 fitted-response values stored in the authors’ original MATLAB figures**, with maximum absolute error **8.88 × 10⁻¹⁵ Hz**. It also independently reconciles the workbook’s 86 fitted means and 86 SEM values with those figures. This passes source-model reproduction; it does not validate plasticity in the MaleCNS runtime or complete A07.1/M7.

The executable is [huang_reference.py](../../research/huang_reference.py), a GPL-3.0-or-later derivative of the original MATLAB code by Junjie Luo, Cheng Huang and Mark J. Schnitzer. Original code, parameter files, article, appendix, data, copyright and full GPL text remain in the preserved source snapshot (original artifact `program/publication-review/huang-luo-sources/README.md`). The four original generated figures were additionally recovered, unchanged, from upstream revision `5d7c08a9a88f923169a0c3008aca68af421e9a7f`; their separate manifest (original artifact `program/m7/huang-reference/sources/generated-model-fitting/manifest.json`) records source URLs, Git blob identities, byte counts and hashes.

## What was reproduced

All four `.mat` files contain `mat_lu_cell`, `para_mu`, `para_CI` and `para_rand`, with **no saved forward trajectories**. The independent target is instead the four `17-Apr-2024` generated `.fig` files. Their `graph2d.lineseries` objects contain original fitted predictions; `specgraph.errorbarseries` objects contain recorded means and SEM. The checker validates the red/blue CS+/CS− order and six x coordinates, then compares every displayed curve point. The fixed comparison tolerance is 10⁻¹⁰ Hz. Every simulated bout’s final response also satisfies the clipped steady-state equation within that tolerance. This is a comparison against independently stored author outputs, rather than a regression file generated by this port.

Two source protocols are deliberately retained. `fitting` follows `model_fitting/fit_nonlinear_models.m` and matches the saved figures. `figure5c` follows `figure_code_examples/plotPanel_5c_S10j_R2_CI.m`, including its four 300-s pauses and different rest durations. The latter uses the March 2023 parameter files selected by that script. March 2023 and April 2024 `para_mu` values are identical for each model, but their saved confidence intervals and random parameter samples differ. This reference uses the unchanged point estimates only. It does **not** claim to reconstruct the paper’s 10,000-sample median/16th–84th percentile Figure 5c curves.

For the original three-module fitting protocol, prediction RMSE is 3.276 Hz across 52 attractive-pair observations and 3.808 Hz across 34 repulsive-pair observations. These are the same 86 observations used by the authors to fit the model. Their error bars do not turn this comparison into held-out biological validation. The original model’s visible residual errors are preserved.

## Source and equation correspondence

The [original publication](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07819-w) describes three MB modules, six DAN/MBON readouts, two abstract KC channels and a shock-sensing input. Its computational appendix (original artifact `program/publication-review/huang-luo-sources/publisher/41586_2024_7819_MOESM3_ESM.pdf`) distinguishes the full continuous formulation from the bout recurrence actually supplied in MATLAB. This port implements the latter.

| Python owner | Original owner and retained operation |
|---|---|
| `parameters` | `parameter_vec2mat.m`: column-major logical assignment of fitted parameters into the saved constrained matrices |
| `protocol` | `experimental_condition.m`, plus the exact fitting/Figure 5c script overrides: 5-s imaging odors, 30-s training odors, 120/135-s intervals, two groups of three training bouts |
| `responses` | `solve_DAN_MBON` in `Dx_steady_state_MBON_0301_2023.m`: six responses relative to baseline, linear DANs and clipped MBONs; retains even the original untransposed initial solve, followed by ten transposed recurrent updates |
| `simulate` odor state | Source adaptation rate 0.05 s⁻¹, exponential recovery, and the start/end arithmetic mean used for normalized KC drive; appendix equations 5.2–5.4 |
| `simulate` efficacy | Source `DW_KM` update, scaling each bout by duration/(3 × 30 s), effective KC→DAN and MBON→DAN contributions, and punishment coupling; appendix equations 5.8 and 5.18 |
| `simulate` decay | Separate γ1 and α2/α3 memory constants, with the latter switching after three hours measured from the end of the last training interval; crossing bouts split exactly at that boundary |
| `observations` / `original_curves` | `load_original_data.m` / `plot_Dx_data.m`: original numeric mean/SEM cells, axis selection, CS order and fit curves |

In the source’s notation, the effective increment is the normalized KC drive multiplied by the sum of an odor-dependent term scaled by `fw0` and the punishment term scaled by `fwDt`. It then decays exponentially and is added to the initial effective KC→MBON weight. No direct anatomical synapse is created or changed. The stored firing response is computed **before** that bout’s efficacy update, while `weights_after` records the subsequent state. Thus the final weight change after the last imaging bout includes that test’s own plasticity; it must not be presented as an undisturbed pre-recall memory measurement.

Unchanged source constants include punishment response increments `[27.85, 0, 11.38]` Hz; MBON baselines `[35.2, 9, 11.2]` Hz; maxima `[71.66, 17.9, 31.16]` Hz; and 20-s KC adaptation. The three-module fitted decay constants are approximately 2,023 s for γ1, 6,220 s for α2/α3 before three hours, and 242,788 s thereafter; KC recovery is approximately 792 s. They are effective fitted model parameters, not measured MaleCNS per-synapse molecular constants. The source’s 35.2-Hz MBON baseline comes from its separate modeling subset and is not replaced with the 37.16625-Hz mean of Source Data Fig. 1.

## Bounded controls

Controls use the original three-module attractive-pair point estimate and Figure 5c schedule. Paired and shifted conditions both span 89,520 model seconds and contain 180 seconds of normalized shock input. For the shifted condition, each 30-s punishment bout is moved into the final 30 s of the following odor-free interval; splitting that interval preserves total time and odor recovery. This is a **bout-level nonoverlap control**, not a quantitative prediction for arbitrary CS–US delays. The original fitted pairing coefficient already represents a fixed 3-s onset offset.

| Condition | γ1 CS+ response after six bouts | α3 CS+ response after six bouts | α3 CS+ response at nominal 1 h |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Paired | −8.408 Hz | −11.032 Hz | −8.071 Hz |
| Frozen efficacy, adaptation retained | 11.644 Hz | 7.481 Hz | 14.397 Hz |
| Odor only | 15.781 Hz | 19.960 Hz | 19.960 Hz |
| Shock only, no odor at any stage | 0 Hz | 0 Hz | 0 Hz |
| Shifted shock, same exposure | 15.781 Hz | 19.960 Hz | 19.960 Hz |

Frozen and shock-only conditions have exactly zero effective weight change. Paired, frozen, odor-only and shifted conditions have identical pre-training responses. Shifted and odor-only imaging outputs differ by at most 3.56 × 10⁻¹⁵ Hz, as expected when punishment has no KC overlap in this recurrence. Odor-only is not a no-plasticity control: innate odor valence also changes the effective weights. The frozen condition retains KC adaptation, so post-training response suppression alone cannot be called learning. Shock-only imaging is a zero-input null, not a cue-recall experiment. These checks expose the model’s mechanism without adding arbitrary calcium, receptor or eligibility dynamics.

## Biological transfer and remaining boundary

The experimental preparation was adult female Drosophila, 3–8 days old at surgery, raised at 25 °C. Voltage recordings used 1-kHz imaging. Original training used 30-s CS+ and CS− odors separated by 135 s; reinforcement consisted of 16 electric pulses, 20 V and 0.2 s each, with the first starting 3 s after CS+ onset. Those physical pulses are represented by a normalized punishment variable in the model, not a simulated shock apparatus or tissue field. The physiological observations, exact assay history, effective recurrence and original fit outputs now form an executable reference.

What can transfer immediately is the reference comparison and its controls: preserved source data, units, initial conditions, timing, numerical curves, and the distinction between adaptation, odor-dependent plasticity and shock-dependent plasticity. The model’s effective connections can include indirect effects and can be negative; they cannot be assigned directly to positive anatomical KC synapses. Hemibrain-derived effective topology and female recording preparations do not by themselves calibrate the adult male MaleCNS graph.

The reference supplies no compartment-specific receptor identity, calcium/release chemistry, odor-selective individual KC mapping, arbitrary-time plasticity kernel, natural food-consequence route, persistent neural/body individual, acquired motor advantage or held-out behavioral result. It does not implement extinction/reversal, restore recall, transfer or sequential-learning evidence in the fly. Those M7 gates remain open, as do the upstream sensory, motor and circuit requirements.
