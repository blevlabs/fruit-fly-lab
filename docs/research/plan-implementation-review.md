# Implementation, data, and compute review

This report records a historical study and its inspected model version. Numerical results below are not new package checks; see [current status](../status.md), the , and the [public results index](../../results/README.md). Original artifact labels identify records in the historical provenance archive.

This review evaluates the earlier FlyWire/MN9 prototype. References to its missing motor, sensory and persistence mechanisms describe that historical version; current whole-MaleCNS implementation guarantees are listed separately in the status page.

**Verdict:** the isolated **raw model-generated MN9 spikes → phenomenological NMJ → native activation/force → synthetic lever** prototype has a completed engineering check in the PASS receipt:2–40 (original artifact `mn9-force-replay-check.json`), and its implementation is present in mn9_force_replay.py:19–89 (historical source `mn9_force_replay.py`). This review inspected the source and saved receipt, not a fresh execution. Recorded maximum relative half-step error is **1.36674×10⁻⁵**. It is an integration experiment, not an anatomical muscle-9 reconstruction. The next implementation target is one identified right M9 mechanical model. The executive plan needs to put this milestone and subsequent neural-to-muscle work before mushroom-body learning, growth, evolution, and language. The main constraints are missing physical/circuit specification and causal integration, not the storage needed for one muscle. The unit contract is now resolved by primary FlyGym documentation as mm–g–s; it is not an outstanding blocker.

“Verified” below means inspected source/data or metadata. “Recorded” means a saved receipt, not a rerun. Findings from the four MN9 reports are attributed to those reports; their literature searches and simulations were not repeated. Resource calculations are estimates, and proposed gates are future work. **P1** blocks the corresponding implementation or scientific claim; **P2** must be resolved before reproduction, longer experiments, or scaling. There is no claimed production incident or emergency.

## Prioritized findings and concrete corrections

### 1. Establish the motor reference before behavioral learning claims

The historical prerequisite sequence was isolated MN9 replay, identified M9 mechanics, causal neural/physical coupling, named sensory feedback and site-specific thermal response. Learning, growth, evolution and communication require their own additional mechanisms and controls. Keeping an imposed servo or gait controller fixed does not establish a biological motor pathway.

### 2. P1 — Muscle identity, one-sided coverage, and missing force data must constrain the milestone

**Evidence.** The present actuator is a newly added rostrum hinge and position servo, with fixed extension −1.05 rad, `kp=3`, and force range ±3: [body.py](../../runtime/body.py). Its input is clipped smoothed MN9 rate divided by 80 Hz: worker:124–128 (historical source `live.py`). The [anatomy report](mn9-anatomy.md) supports right MN9→right m9, head-capsule origin and rostral-apodeme insertion, and distinguishes opposing muscles and distal mechanics. Its [identity discussion](mn9-anatomy.md) retains a source-label discrepancy. The [dynamics report](mn9-dynamics.md) lists unmeasured M9 delay, activation, force, and length/velocity parameters.

**Impact.** A synthetic lever result cannot certify anatomical attachments, bilateral function, retraction, full PER, or feeding. Fitting the old approximately 60°/3° outputs would reproduce the deleted servo's choices. The cached annotation's predicted acetylcholine label has confidence 0.439 and no `known_nt`; it cannot override the adult CM9 glutamatergic evidence reported in [dynamics](mn9-dynamics.md).

**Proposed correction.** Name the current result “model-generated MN9 replay through estimated NMJ and synthetic muscle mechanics.” For the subsequent anatomical model, record neuron/side, muscle, attachment frames, hinge assumptions, passive loads, and each parameter's source/range. Keep neural transmission sign and peripheral NMJ identity separately sourced. Permit estimated physical parameters, with sensitivity results and explicit limits. Do not mirror the right neural output or create antagonist activation from MN9 silence. A one-unit protraction experiment can finish without simulating complete feeding.

### 3. Resolved in the prototype; P2 for transfer — Preserve the explicit units and single activation owner

**Evidence.** Live geometry uses mm and the body timestep is 0.0001 s: global settings:9–12 (historical source `mujoco_globals.yaml`). The updated [mechanics report](mn9-mujoco-mechanics.md) resolves the live convention as mm–g–s. I also verified the explicit millimeter/gram statement in the primary tutorial:226 (historical source `1b_advanced_model_composition.ipynb`). Its synthetic probe uses that same convention. The [dynamics proposal](mn9-dynamics.md) includes an activation state, while the native actuator has its own activation ODE and state: [mechanics](mn9-mujoco-mechanics.md).

**Impact.** Misconverting grams to kilograms would change the force interpretation by 1,000×; that ambiguity is now resolved. Feeding an already filtered activation into native muscle activation would silently add another dynamical stage. The completed replay:63–81 (historical source `mn9_force_replay.py`) correctly sends NMJ excitation to native control and reads the native activation state, so duplicate activation is not a finding against this prototype. Reusing the bundled leg asset's 0.1/0.4 ms activation constants at 0.1 ms Euler resolution is unsafe: the mechanics reviewer reports a one-step overshoot to activation 2. The current synthetic fixture instead uses 10/40 ms and RK4; those are synthetic settings, not M9 measurements.

**Proposed correction for subsequent transfer.** Retain the resolved mm–g–s contract: force unit μN, hinge torque unit nN·m. Let the phenomenological NMJ output bounded **excitation** to `ctrl`; let MuJoCo own **activation** in `act`. If an external activation model is later selected, explicitly remove the duplicate native activation stage. Preserve raw event timestamps and dimensional delay parameters. Before live anatomical transfer, convert physical mass, inertia, force, and torque consistently into this documented convention; verify any alternate imported asset separately. Check force decomposition, torque/power consistency, bounded activation, no-input behavior, and step refinement with the installed 3.9 sparse moment API already verified in [mechanics](mn9-mujoco-mechanics.md).

### 4. P1 — Equal timesteps currently conceal blockwise neural/body coupling

**Evidence.** Worker:95–113 (historical source `live.py`) samples the body once, advances 100 neural steps, and aggregates spikes. Lines 124–137 (historical source `live.py`) turn the whole block's output into one motor command, then advance 100 body steps. The the historical README describes matching 0.1 ms timesteps but does not explain this ordering. The executive plan correctly requests within-chunk spike timing, without specifying the neuromechanical schedule.

**Impact.** A neural spike near the end of a 10 ms block contributes to the command applied from the start of that body's block. Meanwhile sensory contact changes are held until the next block. This is a coarse coupling approximation, unsuitable for assigning physiological onset delays or validating fast feedback without convergence evidence.

**Proposed correction.** Keep 10 ms as a transport/display interval only. Define the simulation order using integer step indices: sample current sensory state → integrate neural state → enqueue/receive delayed motor events → update NMJ/excitation and muscle/body state → refresh needed physical observations. Declare whether emitted spikes are timestamped at the beginning or end of a step; the capture script currently uses `step*DT` after the neural update at [capture](../../runtime/capture_mn9.py). The isolated replay now preserves raw event timing on a checked integration grid; its completed status does not resolve the live coupling issue. For future closed-loop integration, use a causal small reference and measure any proposed block reduction against it. Buffered replay can preserve open-loop spike timing; it does not by itself validate body-to-brain feedback. Measure CPU/GPU synchronization cost before claiming real-time operation.

### 5. P1 — Full motor pathways require a separate data and import gate

**Evidence.** [body.py](../../runtime/body.py) constructs `HybridTurningController` with `PreprogrammedSteps`; worker:129–136 (historical source `live.py`) drives it with a rate-to-CPG conversion. [arena.py](../../runtime/arena.py) scales P9 by DNa02 imbalance and contains no premotor/motor-neuron or muscle connections. The brain-only limitation is explicit in the historical README; MaleCNS is only reviewed, not installed, at [lines 249–269](../../README.md). The executive plan itself warns against silently migrating specimens.

**Impact.** Routing another active descending neuron into the same gait generator does not fill the missing VNC. In the saved odor condition, DNa02 activity with zero P9 gives zero drive; that is a legitimate result of this particular interface. It is not a reason to add forward drive or fit attraction. Joint state returned by MuJoCo is not an identified proprioceptive afferent.

**Proposed correction.** After M9, build one explicit coverage table for the intended full scope: sensory cell → brain pathway → descending neuron where applicable → premotor/VNC circuit → motor neuron → muscle → joint/contact → named afferent. Each row needs specimen/release, IDs, edge evidence, units/delay, and unresolved links. Integrate one documented chain first, then extend the same method; completion requires the declared coverage, not one successful chain. Missing data require an explicit cross-dataset correspondence/model decision, not invented edges. Keep the preset-gait path outside new mechanistic acceptance. A fixed/tethered preparation is a permissible test boundary, not evidence of locomotion.

### 6. P1 — Resume and individual isolation have no implemented state contract

**Evidence.** Worker:51–64 (historical source `live.py`) seeds globally and recreates the five Eon recurrent tensors plus body/controller/readouts. Worker:140–152 (historical source `live.py`) returns pose/velocity and summaries, not a restart image. Viewer:121–129 (historical source `live.py`) closes the worker on exit. The executive plan requests neural/chemical checkpoints, but needs an explicit body/environment/NMJ complement.

**Impact.** Between-frame recurrence is not durable persistence. Saving `qpos/qvel`, rate summaries, or only a PyTorch `state_dict` would omit state used here. The upstream Eon recurrent tuple is passed explicitly; its weight tensor and refractory configuration are plain attributes. A future exact restore, learning trial boundary, and fresh offspring are different operations.

**Proposed correction.** Before learning or population evaluation, implement one versioned checkpoint at a complete simulation barrier: Eon recurrent state and delays; parameter/weight identity and mutable values; NMJ pending events and states; native muscle activation; body integration state; source/internal-state/task data; readout state when consequential; RNG states; and integer clocks. Use MuJoCo's existing integration-state get/set API rather than inventing a pose serializer. Save model changes separately. Exact body continuation must be tested on the same version/architecture; cross-host equality is not implied by equal dimensions. [MuJoCo 3.9 state and reproducibility documentation](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/3.9.0/computation/index.html#state).

Write checkpoints atomically and retain the previous valid one. Verify continuation across an in-flight spike and nonzero muscle activation, fresh-state reset, and rejection of mismatched identities. The completed isolated replay has no checkpoint interface. This is a declared limitation, not a reason to reopen its integration milestone; continuation becomes necessary when adding persistent/live experiments.

### 7. P1 — Temperature reactivity needs a site-specific sensory path, not a behavioral target

**Evidence.** [arena.py](../../runtime/arena.py) defines a static Gaussian heat field, samples antennae, and converts 30–40°C to 0–200 Hz warmth input. The source permits only 22–45°C at [line 32](../../runtime/arena.py). Taste is a contact bit at 200 Hz regardless of local temperature. The temperature receipt:20–36 (original artifact `temperature-feeding-probe.json`) records about 43.4°C at the antennae and 43 MN9 spikes, not abolished feeding. The [thermal reviewer](mn9-validation-temperature.md) identifies labellar input as a distinct requirement; [gates](mn9-validation-temperature.md) separate thermal feeding from nociception/injury.

**Proposed correction.** Specify which reactivity is being tested: antennal environmental warming, labellar food/contact temperature, or temperature dependence of NMJ/muscle parameters. Give each its own local temperature/time signal, cell mapping, transduction, and evidence. Extend cooling only when its range and mapped circuit are specified. Keep parameters estimated where measurements are absent. Validate identical-spike motor temperature experiments separately from sensory effects. Do not gate MN9, taste, force, or walking by desired acceptance/avoidance, and do not invent a burn threshold. Record negative outcomes and limit claims to the implemented site/pathway.

### 8. Reproducibility requires the complete execution inputs

The earlier recipe omitted two required arena files and described an absent manual-stimulation control. A scientific execution manifest must include every required module, annotation, asset and parameter file, with hashes and a declared protocol. Historical numerical claims remain tied to their original result identities rather than mutable filenames.

### 9. P2 — Available data is enough to prototype, but not to validate the complete mechanisms

**Evidence.** Parquet metadata contains pre/post IDs, pre/post indices, connectivity, derived sign/weight, and a serialized index; no muscle, receptor, delay-distribution, synaptic-coordinate, or physiology columns. The plan acknowledges missing raw study data. The four new reports add anatomy and adult electrophysiology constraints, but do not supply a calibrated M9 spike-to-force dataset: [dynamics](mn9-dynamics.md), [validation acquisition contract](mn9-validation-temperature.md). A completeness CSV alone is insufficient, but ignoring the existing annotated TSV would duplicate completed work.

**Proposed correction.** Use one small parameter/data record for the current motor unit, reusing the existing files: value/range, unit, preparation/age/sex/temperature, primary table/figure, measured/derived/estimated status, and intended fitting versus holdout role. Preserve the known source-label discrepancy. Publish missing quantities as missing; estimated geometry/force may support an exploratory mechanism without waiting indefinitely for new experiments. Claim quantitative biological fidelity only for observables actually identified by independent data. Do not port brain signed weights into physical conductances, infer peripheral NMJ constants from an EM transmitter prediction, or turn model-generated traces into “recordings.” Re-estimate the executive  after the reordered milestone and data gate, not by inheriting the old schedule.

### 10. P2 — Memory feasibility is dominated by representations and recording policy, not neuron count

**Evidence.** The executive  is correct but incomplete as an execution budget. [Batch wrapper](../../runtime/run.py) exposes up to 1,000 seconds and 32 trials. The inspected pinned upstream `run_pytorch.py:416–434,473–501` appends all spike indices/times in memory lists, concatenates them, builds a DataFrame, then writes Parquet. `get_weights` at `285–323` reads the full Parquet even when caches exist, and loads COO before CSR. These are upstream source observations, not a stress-test result.

**Proposed correction.** Keep the current replay's recorded population limited to its motor-unit variables. Before long full-graph work, add selected-cell/event recording with bounded flushes and incremental statistics to the experiment evaluator; keep the upstream baseline intact. Specify output/checkpoint retention and free-space headroom. Permit valid silent runs: the existing wrapper requires `n_spikes>0` at [line 41](../../runtime/run.py), unsuitable for some silenced/no-input controls. Profile actual tensor storage and allocator peaks rather than treating tensor shape or a setup-only `nvidia-smi` reading as a maximum.

### 11. P2 — Evolution/language have sound claim boundaries, but no current learning substrate

**Evidence.** The companion correctly distinguishes clocks, inherited versus acquired state, and neural message selection from an external comparator. Its E1 evaluator presumes learning/recall, while its  can read as though a batch protocol alone supplies that dependency. The current [run.py](../../runtime/run.py) calls one fixed experiment; upstream `TorchModel` shares weights and scalar model parameters across batched trials. There is no implemented online plasticity, outcome/energy model, message action head, or symbolic sensory channel in the inspected execution path.

**Proposed correction.** Reuse simple timing/accounting now; defer building population or communication orchestration. E1's meaningful lifetime evaluation requires the chosen learning mechanism and persistence; a four-candidate/two-generation fixture proves bookkeeping only. Start later with sequential isolated candidates sharing immutable graph data, not mutable learned state, and one reduced communicating pair. Freeze sensory/muscle transfer parameters during evolution of learning rules so selection cannot “improve” behavior by strengthening a motor shortcut. Define message observations/actions and learning rules before estimating L0/L1 delivery. Keep growth, heredity, and cultural transmission separate; do not revive them as prerequisites to MN9.

## Resource and validation budget

All MB/GB below are decimal unless marked GiB. Arithmetic uses N=138,639, E=15,091,983, float32=4 bytes, and the reviewed 0.1 ms neural step. These are storage estimates, not measured allocations or performance forecasts.

| Item | Derived size / cost | Practical implication |
|---|---|---|
| One / six float32 edge states | 60.37 / 362.21 MB | Localized chemistry is inexpensive compared with recording the full brain. It still needs index/copy/temporary-array overhead. |
| Packed sparse matrix | CSR with int64 indices: 182.21 MB; COO: 301.84 MB | Both cache formats can coexist. Observed serialized cache files are about 288/289 MiB; serialized backing storage is not established by the minimal CSR formula. |
| Historical recurrent state | 19 delay slots plus conductance, spikes, voltage, refractory: about 12.75 MB per trial | Excludes rates/counts, temporaries, weights, CUDA runtime, and future chemical/structural state. |
| Connectivity columns when decoded | Eight int64 columns: about 965.89 MB | A roughly 97 MB compressed Parquet is not a 97 MB decoded working set; list construction/caches add more. |
| Dense N×N matrix | 76.88 GB / 71.60 GiB | Exceeds a 24-GiB device. No all-pairs growth search. |
| One float32 value per neuron per step | 5.55 GB per simulated second | Full-rate full-brain voltage/chemistry logging is prohibitive for long experiments. |
| Spike index/time triples | At assumed population-mean 1 Hz, 1,000 s: 3.33 GB for one trial; 106.47 GB / 99.16 GiB for 32 | The larger case requires roughly 100 GiB before temporary allocations, before list/DataFrame/copy overhead. 1 Hz is a scenario, not measured activity. |
| Six edge states for 32 distinct individuals | 11.59 GB | Not a complete population VRAM budget; independent graph values, copies, model state, and growth can consume the remainder. Current Eon batching is not this capability. |
| One muscle replay | A few NMJ/activation scalars and a tiny mechanical system; raw traces are 54 events total | No full brain or GPU is needed to verify this replay. Runtime must still be measured; no new infrastructure is justified. |

The saved GPU summary:7–19 (original artifact `gpu/sugar-f040d088913e/summary.json`) measured **0.39251 wall seconds for 0.1 neural seconds**, plus 4.00674 s setup. That is about 3.93 wall seconds per neural second for that short fixed-model run. The log's 0.69 “GB” reading (original artifact `gpu/sugar-f040d088913e/run.log`) is a device-used snapshot computed in GiB upstream, not a peak-memory trace or an isolated per-candidate allocation.

For scale intuition only, maintaining that rate would make a 60 s trial about 3.93 minutes and a one-hour trial about 3.93 hours, excluding setup/body/chemistry. An illustrative 32 candidates × 10 generations × 60 s lifetime is 19,200 neural seconds and about **20.9 wall hours** before controls. Six conditions and five independent repetitions multiply that illustrative work to about **628 hours**. These are conditional arithmetic examples, not selected study sizes or promises. Estimate real campaigns as `conditions × independent replicates × protocols × integrated duration × measured cost`, including parameter fitting, held-out evaluation, failures, and checkpoints.

Future measurement should separate cold load, neural integration, NMJ/chemistry, CPU physics, synchronization, logging, and checkpoint save/restore. Batching's aggregate simulated-seconds/wall-second metric must not be reported as acceleration of one individual's lineage. A population/pair run needs a separate process and state, with independent mutable state.

For validation, retain the completed replay's NMJ/event checks and the existing isolated mechanics checks; avoid repeating that completed work without a new failure or change. Then add independent biological holdouts when those claims are in scope. Current live.py:141–171 (historical source `live.py`) and [check_arena.py](../../tests/check_arena.py) are response/servo checks; do not require the replacement to reproduce their angles. The live finite check at worker:138–139 (historical source `live.py`) also does not cover all new states or MuJoCo warnings. Require finite/bounded NMJ and activation states, expected clock advance, and no numerical warnings/reset; MuJoCo may reset divergent physics before a later finiteness check. [MuJoCo 3.9 simulation pipeline](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/3.9.0/computation/index.html#simulation-pipeline).
